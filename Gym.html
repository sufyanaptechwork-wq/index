Frequently Asked Questions

What are the opening hours?

Mon-Fri: 5am – 11pm, Sat-Sun: 7am – 9pm. Premium members get 24/7 access with key fob.

Is there a joining fee?

No joining fee for online sign-ups. Limited time: waived initiation fee.

Elite members get 4 guest passes/month. Day passes for $15.
